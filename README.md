# SOLANO CITY

A complete, original top-down open-world crime game built for make.fun's
"Make GTA 6" bounty ($1,666 in USDC). Nothing in it comes from Rockstar
Games: the city, the characters, the vehicles, the story, the art, the
sound and the music are all original.

You are Rico Vega, fresh out of prison and $50,000 in debt. Explore
Solano City — a deterministic procedural city of 9x7 street blocks, five
named districts, a park, a beach and a harbour — on foot and by car.
Steal cars, outrun the police, fight rival gangs, and work through three
story missions: The Getaway, The Package, and the Harbor Showdown.

The city lives: 14 traffic cars follow the roads and turn at
intersections, 26 pedestrians walk the sidewalks and flee gunfire and
crashes. A wanted level of 1–5 stars rises when you steal cars, crash
into things or shoot, and decays while you hide. Police cars chase and
ram you; officers on foot can bust you. Combat is a pistol with mouse
aim, a 12-round magazine and R to reload, against gang members who shoot
back. The HUD shows cash, health, wanted stars, ammo, speed, the current
objective, the time and the district, plus a live minimap with roads,
the player, police and mission markers. Day turns to night, headlights
come on, and a save in localStorage lets you continue where you left
off. All procedural, zero assets, zero network requests. Plays offline
in a desktop browser from its own files.

## Play

Open `game/index.html` in desktop Chrome, or serve the game folder
locally and open it in your browser.

## Controls

- WASD — walk, Shift — run
- E — enter / steal a car, mouse — aim, click — shoot, R — reload
- P — pause, M — mute
